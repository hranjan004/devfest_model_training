# coy product demo — credits and notes

A 90-second marketing edit of real native iOS simulator footage. Single-line feature introductions precede each feature. The original capture has been trimmed, lightly retimed, framed and cross-dissolved; no app screens, model results or saved comments were fabricated. Silent, 1920×1080, 30 fps.

The extended ending browses Ranked, opens Deerly (black-tailed deer) and Whiskers (coyote), and locates each on the map. Their imported demo locations are approximate. Goldie uses an example marked campus location. Posts in this build are saved locally on the device. Photo source credits follow.
